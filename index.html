About

gk

I’m Georgios Kosyfas, a brand identity and web designer based in Athens. I take businesses from a first logo to a complete brand and the website that carries it.

Every project starts from what makes the client different, then runs through every touchpoint: the sign on the door, the menu on the table, the flyer, the virtual tour and the site. I work in Greek and English, and I’m comfortable working internationally.
